    generator -> generator : Apply mutations to non-elite

    note over generator

    end note

    note over generator
    Phase 5: Diversity maintenance
    end note

        generator -> generator : Identify stagnant solutions
        generator -> randomizer : Replace poor performers with new solutions
    end
